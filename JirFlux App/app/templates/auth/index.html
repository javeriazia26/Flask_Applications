Break projects into work people can own.

Every project keeps its progress, deadline and members together, with each work item one click away.

  • Detail where it counts. Priority, dates, assignees and comments live on the work item itself.
  • Progress you can trust. Project status and completion update as work items change.

Roles belong to the project, not the person.

Someone can own one project, contribute to another and only follow a third. JirFlux checks their role in that project before every action, instead of relying on one global admin and user split.

Pick a role to see what it can do.

Role in this project

Runs the project: manages its details and who is part of it.

  • View the project and its work itemsAllowed
  • Add, update and delete work itemsAllowed
  • Edit or delete the projectAllowed
  • Add members and visitorsAllowed
  • Leave the projectNot allowed

Know what changed without asking.

Notifications and the activity feed tell the team when work is created or updated, so nobody has to chase status.

  • Filter the noise. Switch between unread, due today, mentions and system updates.
  • Clear the queue. Mark everything as read in one click.
